# Aaraksha Video: Visual & Character Bible

## Overall Visual Style
**Style:** Cinematic, Painterly 2D animated-film still. Think of the warmth and emotional depth of modern 2D animation, characterized by expressive lighting, rich colors, and detailed, immersive environments. 
**Environments:** Lush Himalayan foothills, terraced fields, dense forests, and vibrant local markets.
**UI Elements:** Flat, clean UI inserted directly into the scene, always distinct from the painterly world to maintain the boundary between the real environment and the digital ecosystem.

---

## 1. Kavya (The Traveler)
- **Role:** Confident solo backpacker experiencing the Northeast for the first time.
- **Silhouette:** Olive green utility jacket, mustard-yellow scarf, a durable hiking backpack. 
- **Palette:** Earthy tones with a pop of mustard yellow, ensuring she stands out against the lush green and misty blue environments.
- **Personality:** Adventurous, open-minded, thoughtful. She respects the mountains and relies on Aaraksha to guide her.

## 2. Tenzing (The Local Guide & Homestay Owner)
- **Role:** A verified local provider who represents the "Digital Ghost" economy now made visible by Aaraksha.
- **Silhouette:** Traditional patterned vest over a simple full-sleeve shirt. Weathered, warm face.
- **Palette:** Deep crimson and earthy browns.
- **Environment:** His homestay is surrounded by traditional handlooms and warm wooden architecture, adorned with the glowing green "Verified" leaf badge.

## 3. Officer Sharma (Government Dispatcher)
- **Role:** The unseen guardian monitoring the Govt Command Center. We only see his hands, desk, and the glowing monitors.
- **Environment:** A dark-ops room. Slate-900 theme, multiple monitors showing a 3D MapLibre terrain map and the live TSI (Travel Safety Index).
- **Palette:** Dark blues, emerald greens (safe markers), and amber (alerts).

## 4. Dev (Citizen Volunteer Rescuer)
- **Role:** The local hero dispatched via the unified rescuer network.
- **Silhouette:** Waterproof riding jacket, helmet, riding a battered but reliable motorbike.
- **Palette:** Dark grays with teal accents (matching the teal volunteer portal theme).

## 5. The Woven Thread (The Metaphor)
- **Role:** The visual embodiment of the Aaraksha ecosystem.
- **Appearance:** A continuous, glowing thread of light.
- **States:** 
  - **Amber/Gold:** Normal planning and routing.
  - **Green:** Interacting with a verified local provider.
  - **Red:** Warning, safety drop, or SOS active.
  - **Teal:** Rescue route active.
