# Aaraksha Video: Prompts for Visual Generation

These Gemini prompts are designed to generate the "Painterly 2D animated-film still" style for the main cinematic video.

### Scene 1: The Dream (Hook)
**Prompt:** A single mote of warm amber light shaped like a glowing thread, awakening on an intricately patterned traditional Northeast Indian wooden handloom. The thread lifts off the fabric and steps into a real misty Himalayan dawn. Wide shot, terraced fields, prayer-flag road disappearing into fog. Painterly 2D animated-film still, indigo-to-gold dawn gradient, cinematic lighting.

### Scene 2: Discover and Plan
**Prompt:** A confident young woman backpacker in an olive green utility jacket, mustard-yellow scarf, and red beanie, typing on her phone at a misty mountain coffee shop. A glowing amber thread of light swirls gently around her device. Painterly 2D animated-film still, morning light, warm and lively, no text in the environment.

### Scene 3: Connect with Local Tourism
**Prompt:** A warm Northeast Indian village homestay in golden late-morning light. An elderly homestay owner in a traditional patterned vest welcomes a young woman backpacker in a red beanie. A small glowing green leaf-badge hovers gently over the wooden homestay sign. A glowing amber thread circles overhead. Painterly 2D animated-film still.

### Scene 4: Destination Intelligence
**Prompt:** A golden-hour mountain road. A young woman backpacker is hiking as dark storm clouds gather rapidly at the edge of the frame. The glowing amber thread of light darting in front of her face turns into a flickering, anxious red-orange. Painterly 2D animated-film still, dramatic warm-to-stormy lighting.

### Scene 5: Offline SOS
**Prompt:** Dramatic dark storm scene, thick fog in a remote Himalayan valley. A young woman backpacker stranded by a minor washout. She presses her phone screen. A brilliant, intense white light (representing an SMS) shoots out from her phone, piercing straight up into the dark, stormy sky. Painterly 2D animated-film still, tense dramatic cinematic lighting.

### Scene 6: The Connected Ecosystem
**Prompt:** Night, heavy rain. A determined young Northeast Indian volunteer rescuer in a waterproof teal-accented riding jacket speeding on a motorbike along a misty mountain road. A glowing teal light traces a precise route on the road ahead of his headlight. Painterly 2D animated-film still, heroic motion-blur lighting.

### Scene 8: Emotional Payoff
**Prompt:** Epic wide shot from behind. A young woman backpacker and a local motorbike rescuer standing together looking out over misty golden Himalayan mountains of Northeast India at calm post-storm sunset. The glowing amber and teal threads merge and rest calmly beside them. Painterly 2D animated-film still, warm emotional golden-hour lighting.
